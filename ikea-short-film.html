IKEA short film · Filmed by Li Yu

Home Is More Than a Place

When you feel alone and far from home, even a warm lamp or a familiar mug can bring a sense of comfort. I filmed this short in an IKEA showroom, using everyday objects and a quiet phone call to show how a space can feel like home.
